## Issuers' own statements set beside the trustee's report

Issuers must give their trustee an auditor-certified statement of financial covenant compliance every quarter, and must keep the compliance reports they file on their own website (regulation 62(1)(g) of the LODR Regulations). The statement is made by a different party from the trustee, but it is not independent of the trustee's report: the trustee receives it, and two trustees' printed definitions date the breach and its detection by the issuer's certificate (see What the date columns mean).

For the quarter ended 2026-06-30, logged searches covered 11 issuers: a statement was found for 3, located but not retrievable by the pipeline for 1, and not found for 7.

Set beside the trustee's report for the same quarter, 17 ISIN-level comparisons agree, 3 differ and 2 cannot be compared because the statement's stated scope does not reach the listed covenant. In 17 of the 17 agreements the trustee dates its detection on or after the date of the issuer's statement or of the issuer's compliance report reaching it, and in 6 the trustee's row names the certificate as its basis. Agreement of this kind shows that the issuer's statement was carried into the trustee's report. It does not confirm the statement independently. The disagreements are where one party's record contains something the other's does not.

## The monitored population behind a breach report

A trustee's breach report is selected on a breach having been reported. Its tables of compliance reports received (Annex-VIIA, Table 3) list the issues it monitors, which gives a denominator for the same trustee and quarter. The tables are not republished here. The dataset keeps each file's hash, the count of ISINs it prints, the sample, and the few rows the analysis cites, and `brdo verify` recomputes the count and the sample from the file. A listing is a trustee's report of a breach, so the share listed is not a breach rate.

catalyst-trusteeship's table of quarterly compliance reports received for the quarter ended 2026-06-30 prints 1832 distinct ISINs that pass the check digit. Its breach report for the same quarter lists 44 ISINs, 2.4% of that number. 43 of them are printed in the census; INE982X08034 is not. A comparison sample of 20 ISINs that the breach report does not list is drawn from the census by a fixed rule, so anyone holding the same file can reproduce it: INE003L07077, INE02KN07071, INE02Y107057, INE0IX207239, INE115A07PY3, INE125T07311, INE134E08IL6, INE134E08JH2, INE235P07548, INE296A08870, INE348L07316, INE432R07265, INE448U07273, INE477L07BC0, INE519Q08186, INE721A07RQ0, INE787H08063, INE814O07592, INE866I07750, INE918K07PU7.
